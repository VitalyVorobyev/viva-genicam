## Endurance

Claude's saved 7200 s run delivered 222,375 complete frames and one incomplete,
with no missed frames or backpressure. It requested and recovered 144 packets;
RSS stayed roughly 5–14 MiB and the open-file count stayed at 15. The raw
record does not establish why one frame was incomplete. A separate 600 s run
delivered 18,531 complete frames without loss. Neither is an eight-hour pass.
Those source reports and resource samples are preserved in `prior-run/`.

The recovered progress output narrows the incomplete frame to elapsed
4742–4802 s: `incomplete` changed from 0 to 1 and `packets_missing` from 0 to
341, while recovered resends increased from 97 to 144. All 144 requested
resends were recovered by the end of the run. In the same reporting interval,
the largest application-observed frame gap rose from 101.018 ms to 134.203 ms,
and the largest device-timestamp interval became 64.757 ms. Resource samples
bracketing it, at 4725 and 4908 s, showed 10,048 and 11,328 KiB RSS with 15
open files in both. The report has no packet capture or receive-arrival timing
series that distinguishes wire loss from host scheduling or buffering; the
cause remains unresolved. These samples do not establish an allocation leak.

completion around 21:49 CET. The first minute delivered 1,854 complete frames
