## Endurance

Claude's saved 7200 s run delivered 222,375 complete frames and one incomplete,
with no missed frames or backpressure. It requested and recovered 144 packets;
RSS stayed roughly 5–14 MiB and the open-file count stayed at 15. The raw
record does not establish why one frame was incomplete. A separate 600 s run
delivered 18,531 complete frames without loss. Neither is an eight-hour pass.
Those source reports and resource samples are preserved in `prior-run/`.

An eight-hour follow-up is prepared in `run_soak.py`. Once started, its durable
`soak-state.json` records the process and phase; `soak-8h.log`,
`soak-8h-resources.jsonl` and `soak-8h.json` hold progress, resource samples and
the final counters. The wrapper prevents idle sleep during the test and attempts
to reload and compare the saved configuration on exit, recording any failure.
Until the final report is inspected, this is pending endurance evidence.

The original configuration is `before.txt`, and the host interface snapshot is
`host-before.txt`. Restoration results must be checked before this run is called
complete. No buffer-pool change is justified by the measurements so far.
